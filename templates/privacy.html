Privacy Policy

Your spending is personal. Here's what we collect, why we collect it, and how we keep it safe.

Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Spendly handles your information when you use our personal expense tracking application. By using Spendly, you agree to the practices described here.

01

Data We Collect

We only collect what we need to run the service:

  • Account details such as your name and email address.
  • Expense data you enter, including amounts, categories, dates and descriptions.
  • Basic technical data such as browser type and session information, used to keep you signed in.
02

How We Use Your Data

Your information is used to:

  • Create and manage your Spendly account.
  • Store your expenses and show you summaries and category breakdowns.
  • Keep the service secure and fix problems.
  • Contact you about important changes to the service or these policies.

We never sell your personal or financial data, and we do not use it for advertising.

03

Data Storage

Your data is stored in a secured database, and passwords are stored only in hashed form, never as plain text. We keep your data for as long as your account is active. You can edit or delete your expenses at any time, and if you delete your account, your associated data will be removed.

No method of storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorised access.

04

Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on a small number of trusted providers, such as hosting services or font libraries, to deliver the app. These providers only receive the information needed to perform their function. We do not share your expense data with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

05

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to request access to or deletion of your data, reach out to us at privacy@example.com. You can also review our Terms and Conditions.
